About GeoSpatial Resources Solutions (GRS)

GeoSpatial Resources Solutions (GRS) is a consulting and research-oriented organization specializing in geospatial technologies, environmental analysis, and sustainable natural resource management.

We integrate GIS, remote sensing, digital soil mapping, and spatial modeling to support decision-making in agriculture, land management, and environmental planning across Africa.

Our mission is to transform spatial data into actionable knowledge for sustainable development.
